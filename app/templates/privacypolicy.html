Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

SpecDrift (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the SpecDrift website and services.

1. Overview

SpecDrift is an API contract comparison tool designed for developers. Our guiding principle is simple:

We do not collect or store your API specifications or personal data unless absolutely necessary.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • User accounts or authentication data
  • Uploaded OpenAPI specifications
  • Stored comparison results
  • Personal identifiers
  • User tracking across sessions

Uploaded files are processed in memory only and discarded immediately after the comparison is completed.

3. How SpecDrift Processes Your Data

In-Memory Processing

When you upload or paste API specifications for comparison, SpecDrift processes them entirely in server memory. No files are written to disk, no databases store your specifications, and no caching mechanisms retain your data. Once the comparison is complete and results are returned to your browser, all data is immediately discarded.

Session Storage

Comparison results are temporarily stored in your browser's session storage to enable navigation to the results page. This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab or navigate away. We do not have access to this data.

No Long-Term Retention

SpecDrift does not maintain any database of API specifications, user uploads, or comparison results. We cannot retrieve, review, or reproduce any specifications you've previously compared.

4. Information We May Collect

a. Technical & Usage Data

We may collect limited, anonymous technical information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • General usage statistics

This data is used solely to improve performance, understand usage patterns, and maintain service reliability. It cannot be used to personally identify you.

b. Server Logs

Server logs may temporarily record request metadata such as timestamps, HTTP status codes, and error messages. These logs do not include uploaded API specifications and are periodically rotated or deleted.

5. Cookies

SpecDrift does not use tracking cookies. If analytics are enabled, they are privacy-focused, cookie-less, and used only for aggregated metrics.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or analytics services) strictly to operate the platform. These providers do not receive your uploaded API specifications and are required to adhere to appropriate data protection practices.

Google AdSense

SpecDrift may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. While the site is awaiting ad-network approval, we do not place ad units on upload, result, contact, privacy, or terms pages.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the service, including in-memory processing and secure server configurations. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted to and from SpecDrift is encrypted using HTTPS
  • No Persistent Storage: API specifications are never written to disk or databases
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect what's absolutely necessary to operate the service
  • Regular Security Updates: Infrastructure and dependencies are kept up to date with security patches

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because SpecDrift does not collect or store personal information or API specifications, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. You maintain complete control over any data you submit to the service by virtue of the fact that we don't retain it.

Ad Settings

If you wish to control advertisement personalization, you can:

  • Adjust your Google Ads Settings
  • Use browser extensions or settings to block advertisements
  • Enable "Do Not Track" in your browser settings

9. International Users

SpecDrift may be accessed from anywhere in the world. By using this service, you acknowledge that any information collected may be transferred to and processed in countries where our servers are located. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place regardless of where data is processed.

10. Children's Privacy

SpecDrift is intended for developers and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Open Source Transparency

SpecDrift is committed to transparency. Our source code is available on GitHub, allowing you to verify our privacy claims and security practices. You can review exactly how your data is processed by examining the code yourself.

Plain-English Summary:
No accounts. No stored API specs. No selling data. No creepy tracking. Just a tool that does one job well.
